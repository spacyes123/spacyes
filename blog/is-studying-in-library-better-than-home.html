STUDY TIPS

Is Studying in a Library Better Than at Home?

For most serious exam aspirants in Mumbai — the answer is yes. Here's why.

The home studying problem

Home is optimised for rest and family life — not deep work. Interruptions from family, background TV noise, the temptation to lie down, easy access to food and phone — all of these fragment your attention. Most students studying at home report 2–4 actual focused hours out of 8 hours sitting at their desk.

What changes in a library

A dedicated study library removes every variable that competes for your attention. Silent zones enforce discipline. Other students create social accountability. A fixed desk creates a mental anchor — your brain learns to associate that chair with focused work.

The numbers from Spacyes

Members at our Kandivali and Borivali branches average 6–7 focused hours per day — nearly double what most report studying at home. The environment does the heavy lifting.

When home studying works

Home works for revision of familiar material, light reading, and winding down study sessions. It's rarely effective for first-time learning of complex concepts or timed practice.

The hybrid approach

Many Spacyes members use a hybrid model — library for their core 6–7 hour study block, home for 30–60 minutes of light revision before sleep. This combines the best of both environments.
