Best Study Library in Kandivali West, Borivali West & Kandivali East

If you've typed "library near me" into Google more than once this month, you already know the problem: most study spaces in Mumbai are either too noisy, too crowded, or don't have the hours you actually need. Here's an honest, locality-by-locality look at all three Spacyes branches — so you can pick the one that's actually closest to where you live, study, or coach.

Spacyes Kandivali West — Mahavir Nagar

Tucked into Mahavir Nagar, this branch sits behind Nandu Dosa at Pragati Society, Modi House. If you're a regular at the Mahavir Nagar food street or live anywhere around Charkop, Poisar, or near the Mahavir Nagar metro station, this is the closest premium study option to you.

Nearby landmarks: Mahavir Nagar, Behind Nandu Dosa, Pragati Society, Modi House.

Also serves: Charkop, Poisar, Akurli, Mahavir Nagar metro, DN Nagar.

Students here are typically prepping for banking exams, CA, and state-level competitive exams — the silent reading hall and 24×7 access (on 7+ day plans) make late-night and early-morning sessions realistic, not just theoretical.

Spacyes Borivali West — SV Road

This branch sits on SV Road, right opposite Kalyan Jewellers and next to the Shiv Temple, inside Bharat Baug at Lancelot. It's a short walk from Borivali station, which makes it a popular pick for students commuting in from IC Colony, Eksar, or along LT Road and Chandavarkar Lane.

Nearby landmarks: Lancelot, SV Road, Opp. Kalyan Jewellers, Next to Shiv Temple.

Also serves: IC Colony, Eksar, LT Road, Chandavarkar Lane, Borivali station.

Because it's so close to the station, this branch sees a steady mix of working professionals studying after office hours and students heading straight here after college or coaching classes nearby.

Spacyes Kandivali East — Thakur Village

Located inside Singh Estate, directly opposite Gate 6 of Thakur College, this branch is built for the Thakur Village crowd — students already walking distance from one of Mumbai's biggest education hubs.

Nearby landmarks: Thakur Village, Opp Thakur College Gate 6, Singh Estate.

Also serves: Thakur Complex, Lokhandwala Township, Akurli Road, Mahavir Nagar.

If you're enrolled at Thakur College or any of the coaching institutes around Thakur Complex, this branch saves you the commute entirely — many students walk over between classes and study sessions.

So, which one should you pick?

Honestly — whichever one is closest to your daily route. All three branches offer the same core experience: silent study zones, high-speed WiFi, air conditioning, RO water, and a strict no-phone policy that keeps distractions out. The real differentiator is proximity. A library you can walk to in five minutes will always beat a "better" one that's 40 minutes away on a bad day.

If you're on a 7-day or longer plan, you're not locked into one branch anyway — you can study at Kandivali West one day and Kandivali East the next, depending on where your day takes you.
