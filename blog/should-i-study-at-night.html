STUDY TIPS

Should I Study at Night?

The honest answer — plus why some of Mumbai's top exam scorers swear by late-night sessions.

It depends on your chronotype

Some people are naturally night owls — their focus and alertness peak after 9 PM. If that's you, studying at night isn't just fine, it's optimal. The mistake is forcing yourself into a morning routine that doesn't match your biology.

The real problem with studying at night at home

The issue isn't the time — it's the environment. At home, late nights bring distractions: family noise winding down, phone temptations, the bed nearby. Without a structured environment, night studying often turns into browsing sessions that feel productive but aren't.

What a 24/7 study library changes

At Spacyes, members on weekly and longer plans have 24×7 access. This means you can study at 11 PM, 2 AM, or 5 AM in a silent, air-conditioned environment with other serious students around you. The social proof of seeing others study keeps you accountable.

Best practices for night studying

Keep a consistent sleep time. Even if you study late, sleeping at the same time each night preserves your circadian rhythm.

Avoid screens for 30 minutes before sleep. Blue light suppresses melatonin and ruins sleep quality.

Don't pull all-nighters before exams. Sleep consolidates memory — sacrificing it before an exam is counterproductive.
