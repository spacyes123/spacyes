Why Spacyes Co-Working

Not just a desk. A proper work environment.

Focused atmosphere

A quiet, professional environment where you can take calls, attend video meetings, and work without distractions — without the noise of a café or open office.

High-speed WiFi

Reliable, fast internet designed for remote work — video calls, large file transfers, and always-on connectivity without dropouts.

3 branches, 1 plan

Monthly desk holders can use any of the 3 branches — Kandivali West, Borivali West, or Kandivali East — whichever is closest on a given day.

AC + pantry

Air-conditioned throughout. A clean pantry for tea, coffee and water breaks — everything you need for a full productive work day.

No long-term lock-in

Start with a day pass to try it out. Upgrade to monthly when you're ready. No annual contracts, no security deposits.

Corporate-ready

Need multiple desks for your team? We accommodate corporate bookings with the same per-seat pricing — just WhatsApp us your requirements.
