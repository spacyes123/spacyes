House Code

Rules & Regulations.

Spacyes Library runs on a strict no-distraction code. By becoming a member you agree to the following — that's how we keep the silent study environment intact for everyone.

01Fees once paid are non-refundable, non-transferable & non-adjustable.
02Photo & Photo ID must be submitted within 3 days of admission.
03Seat availability is on a first-come, first-served basis.
04Premises entry restricted 23:00–06:30; otherwise open 24×7 for 7+ day plans.
05Members on 7+ day plans can access all 3 locations.
06Mobile phone use is STRICTLY PROHIBITED inside the premises.
07No form of communication is allowed inside the Reading Hall.
08Members must Login & Logout each entry; break time is max 30 minutes.
09Missed logout = membership blocked for the day.
10Maintain correct posture & decorum at all times.
11Eatables, gum, chocolate or drinks NOT allowed inside the Reading Hall.
12Wi-Fi is complimentary on laptops/tablets only (no SIM-slot devices).
13Belongings must NOT be left unattended in the premises.
14Shoes must be kept on the designated rack.
15Spacyes is NOT responsible for loss/damage of personal belongings.
16AC temperature is fixed; do not adjust AC flap manually.
17Group discussions NOT allowed anywhere on premises (one-on-one only, in pantry).
1824×7 CCTV surveillance is in place for your security.
19Smoking is NOT permitted on premises.
20Spacyes reserves the right to terminate any membership without notice or refund.
21Cheque bounce charges: ₹350 + bank charges. Card payments: +2.5%.
22Subject to Mumbai jurisdiction. Rules may change without prior notice.

* Spacyes reserves the right to modify these rules without prior notice. Subject to Mumbai jurisdiction.
