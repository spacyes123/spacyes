Who Studies Here

Built for every serious aspirant.

UPSC & Civil Services

Long prep windows need consistent, silent environments. Spacyes members on yearly plans study here 8–12 hours daily through their full preparation cycle.

CA & CFA Students

Articleship leaves little time — Spacyes gives CA aspirants a focused space for those critical evening and weekend study hours.

JEE & NEET

Timed practice, mock tests, and deep revision sessions need a space that matches exam-hall conditions. Spacyes delivers that daily.

Banking & Government Exams

SBI PO, IBPS, SSC — our most common exam types. The silent, distraction-free environment is the single biggest reason members clear exams here.

MBA & Postgrad

College Students
