ABOUT SPACYES

Every great achievement begins with a focused mind.

At Spacyes, we believe that success isn't just about studying harder—it's about studying in the right environment. In today's world, finding a quiet, comfortable, and distraction-free place to focus has become increasingly difficult. Homes can be noisy, cafés are often crowded, and public spaces rarely provide the peace students and professionals need.

That's why Spacyes was created.

Our journey began in 2015 with a simple vision: to provide a premium study space where anyone with a dream could work towards it without distractions. What started as a single library has grown into multiple locations across Mumbai, serving thousands of students, working professionals, freelancers, and lifelong learners.

Over the years, Spacyes has become more than just a library. It has been a silent partner in countless success stories—students preparing for competitive exams, professionals upskilling for better careers, entrepreneurs building businesses, and individuals pursuing personal goals.

"Every desk has a story. Every seat has witnessed determination. Every hour spent here is an investment in someone's future."

OUR TIMELINE

10 years. 3 branches. One mission.

2015 — Kandivali West

Our first branch opened in Kandivali West — a small, silent study space built for serious students who had nowhere to go.

2016 — Borivali West

Demand from students across the suburb led to our second branch at Lancelot Building, SV Road, Borivali West.

2020–2022 — COVID years

Enrolments dropped sharply as students moved online. But Spacyes stayed open — for those who still needed a place to think, read, and prepare.

2022 onwards — Recovery

As exams returned, so did Spacyes. Members who had studied with us before CA Finals, UPSC Mains, and JEE Mains came back — and brought others.

2025 — Kandivali East

Our third branch opened at Thakur Village, Kandivali East — serving students from Thakur Complex, Akurli, Magathane and surrounding areas.

We continuously improve our spaces to ensure members have everything they need to stay productive, including high-speed Wi-Fi, air-conditioned study areas, comfortable seating, charging points, pantry facilities, CCTV security, and flexible membership plans designed around different schedules.

But what truly sets Spacyes apart isn't the facilities—it's the community. Every member who walks through our doors shares a common purpose: to grow, learn, and achieve something meaningful. That shared dedication creates an atmosphere where focus becomes natural and motivation is contagious.

OUR MISSION

To create Mumbai's most trusted study and productivity spaces, where every dream gets the environment it deserves.

Whether you're preparing for your next exam, building your career, working remotely, or simply looking for a place where you can concentrate without interruptions, Spacyes is here to support your journey.

Because when the environment is right, extraordinary things become possible.
